## Goods and specialties

- Raw goods and materials include food, sugar, tobacco, cotton, furs, lumber, ore, silver and horses. Fish contribute food. Manufacturing turns lumber into hammers, ore into tools, tools into muskets, sugar into rum, tobacco into cigars, cotton into cloth, and furs into coats.
- Outdoor specialties include expert farmer, fisherman, fur trapper, silver miner, lumberjack and ore miner, and master sugar, cotton and tobacco planters. Manufacturing specialties include master carpenter, blacksmith, gunsmith, distiller, weaver, tobacconist and fur trader. Other in-scope specialties include preacher, seasoned scout, hardy pioneer and veteran soldier.
- The reference charts give ship movement, cargo capacity and strength, fortification bonuses, skill and school requirements, building costs and prerequisites, and terrain movement/defense/yield values. Their rows belong to the systems above. The guide sometimes presents a rule in both prose and chart form.
- Reports cover colonies, labor, religion, economy, naval strength and foreign affairs. Options include display preferences, keyboard/mouse shortcuts, and notification switches.
