# v018 Survey and Industry

Eight scenarios reached 200 turns. Rivals founded 94 settlements, assigned 72 coastal surveys and dispatched 65 settlement expeditions. Factions saw 47 to 214 tiles. Unreachable destinations abandoned after dispatch fell from 13 in v017 to 0.

New industry produced 4988 Tools, 8843 Cloth, and 2488 Hammers. Tools are available for the producing colony's construction. Cloth can travel on existing freight runs. These chains use aggregate local supply and labor. They have no inter-colony tool deliveries.

Rivals retain surveyed tiles and restrict follow-up settlements to surveyed, reachable sites. Initial landfall remains scripted, and resource scoring still uses the full work radius around a surveyed site. The construction AI chooses one funded project at a time.
