# Historical anchors for Indigenous settlements

The 58 x 72 map supports regional placement. The starting coordinates, settlement counts and production values are provisional scenario data. Group labels represent communities at different political scales. Fictional settlement names are generated from the group name and a number.

| Group | Regional anchor and game bonus | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Innu | Quebec and Labrador interior, with travel camps and a furs bonus | [Parks Canada Meshikamau-shipu route](https://parks.canada.ca/culture/designation/evenement-event/meshikamau-shipu) |
| Mi'kmaq | Atlantic Northeast, with a fish weir and food bonus | [Parks Canada Mi'kmaw territory and fisheries](https://www.canada.ca/en/parks-canada/news/2016/02/18th-century-mi-kmaw-french-alliance.html) |
| Haudenosaunee | Inland Northeast, with Three Sisters gardens | [Oneida Indian Nation on the Three Sisters](https://www.oneidaindiannation.com/the-legend-of-the-three-sisters) |
| Caddo | Southern river country, with river gardens | [National Park Service Caddo history](https://www.nps.gov/articles/series.htm?id=6A63544D-DD3C-5EBB-65A42A51690FB9DC) |
| Pueblo communities | Southwest, with irrigated fields | [Indian Pueblo Cultural Center on Pueblo homelands](https://indianpueblo.org/new-mexicos-19-pueblos/) and [agriculture](https://indianpueblo.org/faqs/) |
| Taíno communities | Caribbean islands, with cassava gardens | [Smithsonian National Museum of the American Indian gallery guide](https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360/pdf/Taino-Gallery-Guide-English.pdf) |
| Mexica | Central Mexico, with chinampa gardens | [INAH on the chinampa system](https://www.inah.gob.mx/boletines/el-sistema-chinampero-de-la-cuenca-de-mexico-en-la-nueva-edicion-de-arqueologia-mexicana) |
| Maya communities | Yucatán and nearby lowlands, with milpa fields | [INAH on pre-Hispanic Maya household production](https://www.inah.gob.mx/boletines/analizan-el-aprovechamiento-de-los-suelos-de-las-unidades-domesticas-mayas-prehispanicas) |
| Tawantinsuyu | Andes, with terrace fields | [Smithsonian National Museum of the American Indian on Inka terraces](https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360/inka-water/agricultural/agricultural) |

The horse model is based on the distinction between a European introduction and subsequent spread through Indigenous exchange networks. The year gate and contact durations are game choices. The model simplifies access and treats some Spanish AI settlements as horse sources. The weapons available in this build are a gameplay restriction.
